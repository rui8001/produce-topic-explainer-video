# Topic to script

## Lock the promise

From the topic, determine:

- the audience and what they already believe;
- the specific question or decision the video will resolve;
- the useful promise delivered by the end;
- the claims that require current facts, product evidence, numbers, dates, rankings, or quotations;
- prohibited claims and the boundary of inference.

## Research

Prefer primary and authoritative sources. For every material claim, record the source, date, claim, usable inference, and needed visual evidence. Current or unstable facts must be verified at production time.

Do not turn a marketing page into independent proof. Do not infer adoption, performance, market position, causation, or user outcomes without evidence.

## Spoken structure

A useful default is:

1. concrete conflict, surprise, or question;
2. immediate answer or reframe;
3. real evidence or example;
4. a simple model that connects the evidence;
5. consequence, decision rule, or action.

Change this structure when the topic benefits from a different order. Keep every section necessary to the promise. Remove throat-clearing, duplicated conclusions, production language, and generic motivational filler.
