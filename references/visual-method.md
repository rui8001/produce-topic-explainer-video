# Visual method

## Three semantic roles

- `persona`: presenter, lived situation, attitude, action, or human consequence.
- `evidence`: authentic screenshots, recordings, documents, data, quotations, or demonstrations.
- `model`: comparison, path, flow, hierarchy, causal chain, matrix, funnel, timeline, or decision rule.

Text is limited to captions, brief approved semantic labels, chapters, and conclusions. It is not a fourth high-frequency visual role.

## Persistent world

Choose one brand-owned visual world for the episode. Evidence windows, model boards, presenter, captions, empty areas, and transitions should feel like parts of that world rather than unrelated templates.

When the presenter remains visible beside changing information, prefer a large information area plus a smaller presenter anchor. The presenter must not cover evidence or captions. This is an information-layout method, not permission to copy a private character or set.

## Meaningful motion

Complete every hero frame before authoring entry motion. Use motion to reveal, compare, select, connect, verify, or conclude. Set initial position, opacity, and stacking before a transition begins. Do not rely on random values, infinite loops, or browser defaults that can appear for one frame.

Persistent captions should switch atomically at real audio boundaries. Evidence video should have an authentic first-frame poster so decoding never exposes a black or empty window.
