# Sample and final QC

## Facts, rights, and privacy

- Every material claim is traceable and used within its allowed inference.
- Missing evidence remains visible as a production gap.
- Reference works influence only general structure or principles.
- Screens, accounts, documents, notifications, and source media pass privacy and rights review.

## Visuals and text

- Presenter identity and visual world remain consistent.
- Generated images contain no prompt text, random words, logos, watermarks, extra characters, false transparency, or evidence claims.
- Visible generated copy equals locked captions plus explicitly approved semantic labels.
- Captions clear the main content and platform safe areas.

## Frames and transitions

- Scan all frames for black, white, or one-frame full-screen spikes.
- Review consecutive frames at every scene and presenter-action transition.
- Review every caption boundary in the caption region for empty frames, double text, background jumps, and one-frame flashes.
- Evidence windows contain authentic content from their first visible frame.

## Audio and delivery

- The full file decodes without audio or video errors.
- Audio duration, caption end, and picture timeline agree.
- Final resolution, frame rate, codec, subtitle file, source/QC record, deviations, and rollback path are documented.
