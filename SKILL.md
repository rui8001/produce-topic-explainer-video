# Produce Topic Explainer Video

Accept a clear topic as the minimum input. Optional inputs are angle, audience, platform, duration, required points, prohibited claims, brand profile, presenter, and voice. Do not require the user to prewrite a script.

For a new topic, read [references/topic-to-script.md](references/topic-to-script.md), then use [references/production-workflow.md](references/production-workflow.md). For an existing episode, read its production status first and resume from the recorded checkpoint.

Read [references/visual-method.md](references/visual-method.md) before locking the visual plan or composition. Read [references/qc.md](references/qc.md) before approving a sample or final render. Use [references/artifacts.md](references/artifacts.md) when creating or repairing an episode structure.

## Preserve the method, not a private project

The reusable method is:

- a strong question, conflict, or contrast in the first seconds;
- an immediate useful answer rather than a vague setup;
- independently verified claims and real evidence;
- one understandable model that organizes the topic;
- a presenter or persona, evidence, and model living in one coherent visual world;
- continuous narration aligned from real audio;
- a representative sample before the final render;
- DOM/text, subtitle-region, full-frame, and transition-frame checks where the composition supports them.

Do not copy a private episode's facts, product footage, account, character, voice, brand name, exact scene sequence, or source files. A user's own brand overlay may lock those choices for their project.

## Produce from the topic

Research claims that affect the video's promise. Prefer authoritative or primary sources and record what the narration may and may not infer. Mark missing evidence `needs_asset`; do not create fake interfaces, metrics, quotations, or user cases.

Write for spoken delivery: concrete, confident, and free of template-like “first/second/finally” scaffolding. Each middle section should add either evidence, a model relation, or a useful consequence. End with an executable action, decision rule, or precise next question.

Generate or import the narration as one continuous piece, then align captions from the accepted audio. Plan shots by semantic phrase rather than punctuation. Every long shot must change meaningfully at narration nodes; background loops and breathing motion do not count.

Build a sample up to 60 seconds that includes all active visual roles and a real transition. Final rendering requires recorded sample approval. Publication is always separate authorization.

Update the status after each stage with locks, sources, checks, missing assets, authorization, next action, and rollback. Keep private production files outside the installed Skill.
